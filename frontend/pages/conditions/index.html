Overview

How common is mental illness in the U.S.?

  • 1 in 5 adults experience mental illness each year
  • 1 in 20 live with serious mental illness
  • 50% of cases begin by age 14
  • Only 47% of those affected receive treatment
  • Suicide is the 2nd leading cause of death ages 10–34
Source: NAMI — Mental Health By the Numbers ↗
