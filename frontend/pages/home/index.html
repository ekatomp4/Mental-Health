The Treatment Gap

Most people with mental illness never receive treatment. The gap varies significantly by age group — and younger adults are least likely to get help.

  • Only 49.9% of young adults (18–25) received treatment in 2024
  • 32.2% of young adults experienced mental illness in 2024
  • Depression & anxiety cost the global economy $1 trillion in lost productivity yearly
  • Serious mental illness causes $193.2B in lost U.S. earnings annually
Source: NAMI — Mental Health By the Numbers ↗
